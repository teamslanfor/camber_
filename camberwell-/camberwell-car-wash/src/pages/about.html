About Camberwell Car Wash

A local hand car wash and valeting business in Camberwell, south east London.

Who we are

Your local hand car wash

Camberwell Car Wash is based under the railway arches at 350 Camberwell Station Road, just round the corner from Denmark Hill station.

We wash and valet cars, vans and 4x4s by hand for drivers across Camberwell, Denmark Hill and the surrounding area. Whether you need a quick wash before the weekend or a full valet before selling your car, we’ll take care of it.

We keep things simple: clear prices, careful work and a car you’re happy to drive away in.
